# Staff of Travelling

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <RecipesFor id="staff_of_travelling" />
</Column>

The staff of travelling uses energy to teleport small distances. The tool can be charged in for example the <ItemLink id="wired_charger"/>.
When aiming at a <ItemLink id="travel_anchor"/>, no power is used and the player will teleport to the top of the anchor.
